The Creality Ender-3 V3 SE is the lab's 3D printer for functional robot parts. The labs here cover setting the printer up, calibrating it and printing parts that fit the boards and sensors used elsewhere on the site. The nozzle and build plate get hot and the axes move without warning, so read the safety notes at the top of each lab before you touch the printer.
