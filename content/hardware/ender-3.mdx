The Creality Ender-3 V3 SE is the lab's 3D printer for functional robot parts. The labs here cover setting the printer up, calibrating it and printing parts that fit the boards and sensors used elsewhere on the site. The nozzle and build plate get hot and the axes move without warning, so read the safety notes at the top of each lab before you touch the printer.

## Setup

Follow the user manual from Creality for unpacking and assembly. The manual then walks through the first start: running the printer's automatic leveling from the "Leveling" menu, preheating from the "Prepare" menu, and installing and using slicing software. The labs below add the checks this lab expects before a student prints.

## Calibration

Calibration here means checking that the first layer sticks and that printed parts match their drawn size. The calibration lab lists the checks, and the Creality wiki has troubleshooting pages for problems such as a first layer that does not stick or leveling data errors. This site does not state calibration numbers; use the values the manufacturer gives.

## Recommended settings

This hub gives no temperatures, speeds or other print settings, because none have been checked against the manufacturer's documents for the lab's filament. Use the slicer's default profile for the Ender-3 V3 SE and the Creality material guide for the filament in use. Settings will appear here only with a source.

## Common robot parts

The labs print parts that hold boards, sensors and motors, such as mounts and brackets. Each part's lab lists its files, its parts and the references it relies on.
