Arduino boards are microcontrollers: small computers that run one program, start instantly and talk to electronic components through their pins. In EdgeMind Lab they are used for the first electronics exercises and wherever a project needs simple, predictable control of sensors and actuators. Programs, called sketches, are written and uploaded from the Arduino IDE.
