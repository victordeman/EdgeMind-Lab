Arduino Fundamentals is the entry point for students who have not built a circuit or programmed a microcontroller before. Each lab adds one idea to the previous one: first an output, then an input, then a simple control loop that connects the two. The same pattern of sensing and acting returns in the Raspberry Pi and AI agent paths.
