Robots need brackets, mounts and enclosures that cannot be bought off the shelf. This path teaches students to run the lab's Creality Ender-3 V3 SE safely, calibrate it and print parts that fit the electronics used in the other paths. Printer safety notes appear at the top of every lab in this path.
