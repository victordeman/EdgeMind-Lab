title: Print your first robot part
summary: Slice a small bracket for a robot build, print it on the Ender-3 V3 SE and check that it fits the part it holds.
hardware: [ender-3]
topics: [3d-printing, robotics]
difficulty: intermediate
estimatedMinutes: 120
objectives:
  - Import an STL file into a slicer and choose an orientation.
  - Choose layer height, infill and supports for a functional part.
  - Print the part and remove it from the build plate without damage.
  - Measure the part and compare it with the design.

safety:
  - The nozzle and build plate are hot during and after printing; do not touch them until they have cooled.
  - Keep hands, hair and loose clothing away from moving axes.
  - Use the printer in a ventilated room and do not leave it running unattended.
  - Cut supports away from your body and wear eye protection.
